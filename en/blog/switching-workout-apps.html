Switching Workout Apps Without Losing Your Training History

October 27, 2026

Even when you're unhappy with your current workout app, the biggest thing holding you back from switching is probably losing your history. Months or years of weight progressions and personal records are a valuable asset for keeping your training going. Most workout apps can export your data as a CSV file, and that lets you move your history into a new app.

What is a CSV file?

A CSV is a plain text file of table data with values separated by commas. Typically the first line holds the headings and every line after that is one set. You can open it in Excel or any spreadsheet app. Even when two apps aren't directly compatible, a CSV lets you hand data from one to the other.

What to check before exporting

  • Where the export lives: usually somewhere in settings or a data management menu. Some apps limit it to paid plans
  • The minimum columns: date, exercise name, weight, and reps are enough to reproduce most of your progress
  • Weight units: check whether weights are exported in kg or lbs. Mixing them up throws your weights off by about 2.2×
  • Body records: bodyweight and similar records are sometimes in a separate file

What to watch for when importing

  • Matching exercise names: the same exercise often has different names in different apps, like "Bench Press (Barbell)" versus "Bench Press." Deciding which exercise each one maps to before importing keeps your progress charts continuous
  • Duplicates: if you logged in both apps for a while, make sure the same workout doesn't end up in there twice
  • Odd values: typos like extreme weights or future dates are worth catching and fixing before you import
  • Make it undoable: it's reassuring to be able to check the result and undo the import if something looks wrong

What to check after switching

Once the import is done, compare the progress charts and personal records for a few of your main exercises with your old app. If anything is off, the cause is usually a unit or exercise-matching mistake. With your history in place, you can keep building from where you left off in the new app.
