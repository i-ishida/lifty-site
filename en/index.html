Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can start logging workouts right after installing LiFTY.

Your records are saved on your device, and you can keep using the app without ever logging in. Logging in is optional — it's only needed if you want cloud backup and restore-on-device-change, which are premium plan features.

Where is my workout data stored?

Your workout data is stored on your device by default (the local database is encrypted). Only when you're logged in with a premium plan is it also synced to the cloud in the background.

I use multiple gyms — can I keep weight records separate for each?

Yes. By registering the same exercise separately for each gym, you can keep weight records distinct per location. The free plan allows up to 2 registered locations.

What's the difference between the free and premium plans?

Premium adds cloud sync & backup, unlimited locations, unlimited menus, an ad-free experience, extra color themes, full access to the Analysis tab, and CSV data export. Core features — logging, progress graphs, and the calendar — stay free.

Will I lose my records if I cancel the premium plan?

No, your records stay intact. If your locations or menus exceed the free plan's limit after cancelling, the most recently added ones are archived (not deleted) — your workout history is preserved.

Can I switch the display language to English?

Yes. Go to Settings > Language to switch between Japanese and English within the app, independent of your device's system language.
